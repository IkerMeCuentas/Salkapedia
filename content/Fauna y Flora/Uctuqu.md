El **Uctuqu** es un ungulado omnívoro y semi-domesticado nativo a la región de la [[Costa Dirsiga]]. La especie desciende del *Sus scrofa* terrestre. Las tribus [[Foreni|foreni]] mantienen y pastorean rebaños de uctuqu por su carne, piel y pelaje. Los colmillos uctuqu, de gran tamaño, se utilizan como materia prima para talla de hueso y ceremonias religiosas.

Una especie poco territorial, el uctuqu viaja entre oases geotermales 


[[Coldtusk]] is an omnivorous, semi-domesticated, even-toed hoofed mammal. The species is derived from the Terran *Sus scrofa*. Coldtusk herds are farmed by [[Salkai]] tribes for their meat, fur and hide.

The coldtusk is a migratory species, travelling between [[Oasis|oases]] in search of food. It is adapted for survival in the extreme Salkan plains.

Among their features, the coldtusk is remarkable because of its eponymous tusks, much larger than those of related Terran species. Tusks are used for defence, intra-species combat and mate attraction, but more notably they are used for foraging - coldtusks have been known to turn over rocks significantly over their own bodyweight.

## Coldtusk swineherding

Coldtusk swineherding is a difficult occupation performed by various Salkai tribes accross the plains. As a semi-domesticated animal, the coldtusk is fairly independent from salkan societies. The Salkai protect the herds from predators and care for them, but the herds travel autonomously. Only with great effort are they directed or controlled by the herders.

Protective of their own and fairly wild, interactions with the herd have to be done with great care, and herders must be familiar with and known by the hogs, or they risk being trampled or impaled. Due to the diplomacy and tact required, swineherders camp separately from the rest of the clan, close to their cattle. Butchers often camp opposite of the herds, and descendants of butchers are traditionally not permitted to take on herding duties until the third generation due to superstition.

Younger swineherders are often tasked with gathering and storing coldtusk dung, one of the main sources of fuel for Salkai tribes. This dung is flattened, dried, and burnt to feed the hearths of the tribes.
